# Logo A: Long shadow

![Logo A on dark](../board/logo-a-on-dark.svg)

**The idea.** A small cup, seen from the side, throws a shadow far longer than itself. That only
happens when the light is low: at dusk, or under a lamp set near the table. The shadow is the
*umbra* in Umbrista's name, and the low light is the hour the bar opens. The crema on top is the one
warm spot, catching the lamp.

**Why it fits a night café.** It tells the story in one picture: the light is going, the cup is
still there. It is the most illustrative of the three, so it suits places where the mark is seen
large (the loading screen, the social share image, the menu header), and it gives the 3D scene a
ready-made motif: every cup in the scene can cast the same long shadow.

**At 16 px.** The handle is dropped and the cup becomes a solid bowl with a crema cap over a thick
shadow bar. It still reads as a cup with a shadow, but it is the quietest of the three in a browser
tab.

**Risks.** Pictorial marks age faster than abstract ones, and the long-shadow look was a flat-design
fashion around 2013. Here the shadow is the concept rather than a style, but it is worth judging
whether it reads that way.
