# Logo C: Last light

![Logo C on dark](../board/logo-c-on-dark.svg)

**The idea.** A monogram U, drawn heavy, which is also the profile of a cup. A horizon line crosses
it at the level of the coffee. The last of the daylight sits below the line, inside the cup: the sun
has gone down, and the bar holds the light now.

**Why it fits a night café.** It is the most typographic and the most compact of the three, so it
works as a stamp: on cups, beside the order code in a confirmation, as the app icon, on a receipt.
The horizon line gives the scroll journey a built-in device: the line can sweep down the screen as
dusk falls.

**At 16 px.** The arms and the horizon bar stay crisp because they are straight; the warm fill
shrinks to a small bowl but keeps its colour. It reads clearly as a barred U.

**Risks.** Without its story, a U with a bar looks like a letter from a phonetic alphabet (it is
close to the character linguists use for a central vowel). It relies on the wordmark beside it in
most places.
