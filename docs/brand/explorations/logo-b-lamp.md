# Logo B: The lamp

![Logo B on dark](../board/logo-b-on-dark.svg)

**The idea.** One pendant lamp, switched on: a cord, a conical shade and a cone of warm light. In
the pool of light sits a cup, drawn as a cut-out, so the cup appears as a dark shape inside the
light. Light and shadow are in the same mark: the lamp is the signal that the bar is open, and the
cup is the shadow it makes.

**Why it fits a night café.** The whole brand turns on the moment the lamps come on. This mark is
that moment, and it uses the palette exactly as intended: night ink, with the lamp colour as the one
warm signal. It is also the mark that animates most naturally: the cone can fade up at dusk, or
warm up once on the loading screen.

**At 16 px.** Cord, shade and cone survive as three clear shapes; the cup becomes a dark notch at
the bottom of the cone. It is the strongest of the three in a browser tab because of its large warm
area.

**Risks.** A lamp with a cone of light is a common pictogram. The cup inside the light is what makes
it Umbrista's own, and it must not be lost when the mark is simplified.
