# Cities

The café's hours belong to its city: it opens at that city's sunset and closes at 03:00 café time.
Each city is invented, but placed on real coordinates in a real IANA time zone, so its sunset can be
computed for any day. All three sit between 30 and 50 degrees of latitude, so the sun sets every day
of the year. The sunsets below were computed for 2026 with the standard sunrise equation.

## City 1: Calvessa

A hill port on the Atlantic where the fog rolls in every evening and the trams run all night.

- Address: Rua das Lanternas 14 (Lantern Street)
- Coordinates: 38.81° N, 9.47° W
- Time zone: `Europe/Lisbon`
- Opens: 17:19 in December, 21:06 in June

Fog and trams give the 3D scene its weather and its sounds; the street name ties the address to the
lamp.

## City 2: Port Ellery

A rain-coast harbour where the night ferry crew needs coffee long after the town has gone to bed.

- Address: 9 Tidewater Steps
- Coordinates: 47.95° N, 124.60° W
- Time zone: `America/Los_Angeles`
- Opens: 16:27 in December, 21:21 in June

Rain is the strongest weather for the living background, and the ferry crew gives the bar its
regulars. It has the widest swing in opening times: long winter nights, short summer ones.

## City 3: Santa Velada

A river city where dinner starts at ten, and the night watchmen once called the hours on every
corner.

- Address: Calle del Sereno 880 (a *sereno* was a night watchman)
- Coordinates: 34.72° S, 58.05° W
- Time zone: `America/Argentina/Buenos_Aires`
- Opens: 17:48 in June, 20:04 in December

The southern hemisphere flips the seasons, so its longest nights are in June. A late-dining culture
makes a bar that opens at dusk feel natural.
