# Palette 2: Filament

**The idea.** Petrol, the blue-green of deep water at night, with the pale gold of a bare filament
bulb. Softer, glassier and less expected than Palette 1. Crema is a light biscuit tone used
sparingly.

**Why it fits.** It belongs to a harbour city: rain on glass, fog. The gold is quieter than sodium
orange, so the night feels calmer and the lamp feels like a glow rather than a sign. The day set is
sea-glass grey-green, with the signal darkened to a cold bronze so it still works as text.

| Role | Night | Day |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Petrol `#0A1C20` | Sea-glass `#D6E3E0` |
| Surface | Harbour `#12292E` | Salt `#ECF2F0` |
| Text | Mist `#E1ECE9` | Petrol `#0A1C20` |
| Quiet text | Fog `#8DA9A5` | Kelp `#3E5957` |
| Signal | Filament `#FFD27A` | Cold filament `#7D5100` |
| Crema (sparingly) | `#E2C7A0` | `#7A5E3A` |

Every pair passes WCAG AA, including day crema (4.56:1); the board lists the computed ratios.

**Watch.** At night the filament gold and the crema are close in hue. Crema must stay rare, or the
signal loses its job.
