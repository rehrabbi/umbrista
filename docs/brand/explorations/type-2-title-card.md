# Type 2: Title card

- **Display:** Bodoni Moda, variable (400 to 900, optical sizes), with italic. `@fontsource/bodoni-moda`, OFL-1.1.
- **Text:** Schibsted Grotesk, variable (400 to 900). `@fontsource/schibsted-grotesk`, OFL-1.1.

**The idea.** Bodoni Moda has the hairline-and-heavy contrast of an old film's title card, and its
optical sizes keep the hairlines safe at large sizes. Schibsted Grotesk is a sturdy grotesque made
for a newspaper, plain and dependable underneath.

**Why it fits a night café.** It leans into "cinematic": the chapters of the scroll journey can open
like title cards. It is more formal and more romantic than Type 1. A serif display is a deliberate
choice here, for the film-title mood.

**Kinetic use.** The italic moves well in a slow rise and fade; it suits fewer, larger words.

**Watch.** High-contrast hairlines can shimmer on low-resolution screens against dark backgrounds,
so the display should stay large (above about 32 px). Schibsted's tabular figures also space out
colons and full stops, so tabular numbers must be applied to the digits only, not to whole lines.
