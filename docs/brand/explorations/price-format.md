# Price format

**Proposal.** An invented currency, the **crown**, written as a word and never as a symbol.

- Menu: the number only, two decimals, tabular figures: `4.60`.
- Totals and confirmations: the number and the word: `19.10 crowns`.
- One key line at the top of the menu: "Prices in crowns, the money of Calvessa. Nothing here is for
  sale."
- Prices step in tenths. No `.99` endings.

**Why an invented currency.** A real currency (euros for Calvessa, dollars for Port Ellery, pesos for
Santa Velada) would place the fictional city in a real country and make the prices read as a real
offer, which works against the honesty rule: nobody should think they can buy anything here. A
neutral format with no currency at all looks unfinished on a menu.

**Why a word, not a symbol.** Any symbol risks looking like a real one. A plain word cannot be
mistaken for real money, needs no key to decode, and is clear to screen readers: "four point six
zero" on the menu, "nineteen point one zero crowns" on the total.

**Why "crown".** Several real countries have used crowns (koruna, krone), so the word sounds like
money without naming any one country's money, and it works for all three cities.
